Understanding your prescription and using it safely

Helpful reminders for reading labels, following directions and asking your pharmacist the right questions.

Pharmacy•4 min read

What to know

Helpful reminders for reading labels, following directions and asking your pharmacist the right questions.

  • Read the medicine name, strength, dose and directions carefully.
  • Follow the prescribed schedule rather than changing doses on your own.
  • Ask your pharmacist about interactions, side effects and anything you do not understand.
  • Keep medicines stored appropriately and out of reach of children.
A practical reminder: General health information can support informed choices, but it does not replace advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

If you would like to discuss a medicine, health question or pharmacy service, the Kalidad Pharmacy team is available to help with practical guidance.
