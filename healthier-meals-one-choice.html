Building healthier meals one choice at a time

Practical ideas for making balanced food choices part of a routine that works for your household.

What to know

Practical ideas for making balanced food choices part of a routine that works for your household.

  • Build meals around a variety of foods rather than relying on one food group.
  • Make gradual swaps that are practical for your household and budget.
  • Keep convenient, nutritious options available for busy days.
  • Think about overall eating patterns rather than aiming for perfection at every meal.
A practical reminder: General health information can help you make informed choices, but it does not replace advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.
