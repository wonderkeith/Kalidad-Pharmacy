How this service works for you

Our wellness range supports everyday personal care, from personal hygiene and oral care to skincare and body care. We offer products such as soaps, shower gels, deodorants, lotions, creams, moisturisers, sunscreens and other essentials designed to help you maintain healthy skin and feel confident every day. We also provide a selection of baby care products, including gentle baby soaps, lotions, shampoos, wipes, diapers and other essentials to support your baby’s delicate skin and everyday hygiene. Our team can guide you in choosing quality products that suit your personal and family needs.

  • Vitamins, supplements, personal care, and everyday essentials.
  • Friendly guidance on the right product for your situation.
  • Quality products selected for dependable everyday use.

How to access it

Simple steps from request to care

  1. Send us a message on WhatsApp and tell us what you need.
  2. Share the relevant prescription, product details, or preferred service information.
  3. Our team confirms the details, availability, timing, and any next steps with you.
  4. Complete your order or visit the pharmacy as arranged.
