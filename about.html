Our story

Health you can count on, any hour

Kalidad Pharmacy has spent the last five years earning the trust of Fort Portal, one careful dispensing and one honest conversation at a time. We're open around the clock because health doesn't keep office hours — and neither do we.

What started as a small counter in Kisenyi has grown into a full-service pharmacy trusted by hundreds of families every month, without losing the personal attention that got us here.

  • Licensed pharmacists on every shift, day and night
  • Free wellness screening for regular customers
  • Same-day delivery anywhere in Fort Portal
