How can we help?

I answer approved general pharmacy questions. Medical, personal, or unapproved questions go to the Kalidad team for review.

Before you start

Your messages are stored in an anonymous Digital Care ticket so the team can review them. Do not enter names, contact details, passwords, PINs, card or account numbers, or other payment credentials. This chat does not provide medical advice or take payment.

Your question is in the Kalidad team queue.
Keep this chat open or return with the same browser to see a staff reply. For urgent concerns, seek appropriate medical care. Do not send payment credentials.

For symptoms, diagnosis, medicine suitability, dosage, interactions, prescriptions, pregnancy, children, or urgent concerns, please speak directly with a pharmacist or seek appropriate medical care.
