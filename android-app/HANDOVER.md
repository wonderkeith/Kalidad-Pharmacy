# Handover and commercial scope

## Included customer flows
Browse → find a product range → add to enquiry → select collection/delivery enquiry and branch → review/send on WhatsApp → staff confirms stock, price, exact pack, total and timing.

Service → read summary → ask a question → review/send on WhatsApp → staff confirms the next step.

Reminder → enter a label and daily time → enable notifications → receive a private generic alert → open app to view the reminder. Reminders are a convenience and may be delayed by battery policies; they are not appropriate as the only reminder for time-critical medication.

## What the $1,000 client package could include
The branded Android APK, source and build instructions, offline content, enquiry flows, local reminder integration, signing-key handover, actual-device acceptance testing, installation guidance and a defined period of bug fixes. No price or sales result is guaranteed by this build. Agree the scope, maintenance period and payment milestones before selling it.

## Before commercial release
1. Pharmacy owner verifies contact numbers, both branch locations, 24/7 claims, service availability and article/product descriptions.
2. Test on at least two real Android phones, including Android 13+ notification permission, Android 15 layout, background reminders and large font settings.
3. Verify every WhatsApp, browser, dialler and maps handoff with the correct installed external app.
4. Owner receives signing backup privately and retains it for future updates. Do not share the password/key publicly.
5. Verify rights for all reused photographs, logos and catalogue images before licensing the app to another business.
6. Choose direct APK distribution or a Play Store release. Store publication requires an owner-controlled developer account, store assets, policy review and release testing. No store publication was performed.

## Deliberately absent features
No invented product prices, stock badges, payments, order tracking, prescription processing or medical advice engine. There is no live stock backend connection in this app. Website pharmacist chat opens externally. Draft history does not prove a message was sent or accepted. No prescription images or patient records are collected locally.

## Optional extensions, separately scoped
Live pharmacy-managed SKU/pricing/stock API, authenticated account and order history, integrated pharmacist chat, staff-confirmed prescription workflow, verified mobile-money provider, delivery status notifications and administrative tooling. The repository’s digital-care server is a separate backend project: it needs an audited live deployment and verified API contract before connecting those capabilities.
